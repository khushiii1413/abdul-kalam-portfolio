About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He studied Physics and later completed aerospace engineering from Madras Institute of Technology. He worked with DRDO and ISRO and made important contributions to India's space and defence programmes. He was also involved in India's missile development programme and was popularly known as the Missile Man of India. In 2002, he became the President of India and served until 2007. He was very popular among students because of his simple nature and inspiring thoughts. He always encouraged young people to study, work hard and dream big. He wrote many books such as Wings of Fire and Ignited Minds. He received many awards for his contribution to the country, including the Bharat Ratna. Dr. Kalam is remembered as a great scientist, teacher and inspirational personality. His life and achievements continue to inspire students and young people.
